Hedyah Song

About

With over ten years of experience in visual arts and lens-based media, she has developed exhibitions, international programmes and public-facing projects across China and Europe.

Her practice explores exhibitions, residencies and participatory formats as sites of research, ethnographic enquiry and knowledge production, with particular attention to overlooked visual practices and social experiences, and to questions of authorship and representation.

Her experience spans galleries, independent art centres and museums, alongside collaborations with organisations including the National Museum of Ethnology of China, Greenpeace, Ant Group and Douyin. She holds an MA in Curating and Collections from University of the Arts London and is currently undertaking doctoral research at the University of Leeds, bringing anthropological and participatory approaches into dialogue with curatorial practice.
