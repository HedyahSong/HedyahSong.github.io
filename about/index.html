About

Hedyah Song is a Leeds-based curator, exhibition-maker and PhD researcher working at the intersection of anthropology, contemporary art and curatorial practice.

With more than ten years of experience in visual arts and lens-based media, her practice explores how exhibitions, residencies and participatory formats can operate as forms of research, ethnographic enquiry and knowledge production.

Her curatorial work is concerned with forms of visual practice and social experience that are overlooked, marginalised or difficult to accommodate within established art institutions. She develops flexible spaces for experimentation, dialogue and public reflection, with particular attention to authorship, representation and the relationships through which exhibitions are made.

She is currently undertaking doctoral research at the University of Leeds.
