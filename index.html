About

Hedyah Song is a curator, exhibition-maker and PhD researcher whose practice explores how exhibitions, residencies and participatory processes can operate as forms of research and knowledge production. She has more than ten years of experience working with contemporary art, photography and lens-based media.

Selected Projects

EV+1

Inter Art Center, Beijing · 2022–present

A flexible exhibition series supporting emerging artists and established practitioners testing unconventional approaches. The project creates space for visual practices and social experiences that are often difficult to accommodate within larger institutional exhibitions.

Chinese Visual Ethnographic Photo Biennale

2020 and 2022

Curatorial and exhibition work bringing photography into dialogue with visual anthropology, ethnographic enquiry and changing forms of everyday life.

Everyday Lives and Practices Among Women in Nujiang

Ongoing doctoral research

A participatory arts-based study exploring women’s everyday lives in Nujiang, Yunnan, in the context of regional development. The research combines ethnographic enquiry with workshops, residencies and collaborative forms of image-making.
