01

Project EV+1

Inter Art Center, Beijing
2022–present

On-going pet project started on the 7.1-metre wall in the Pixel Bookstore, Inter Art Centre in 2022, aiming to:

  • Provide a ‘debut stage’ for emerging artists yet to gain exposure;
  • Serve as a testing ground for established artists experimenting with new techniques or unconventional ideas;
  • Offer ‘exposure compensation’ for bold and raw works overlooked in competitions but brimming with creative vitality and sincerity.
View Project EV+1 ↗

Project introduction · 02

Due to the small scale and flexible duration of the exhibition, it can to some extent circumvent censorship, allowing works that may not be displayed in larger exhibitions to be presented here.

This project, born in the post-pandemic era, also aims to facilitate authentic face-to-face communication. Each exhibition is accompanied by a sharing session, where each artist is required to attend and engage in discussions. As a result, most of the participating artists are Beijing-based.

Currently, 8 artists have participated in the project, aged between 21 and 35, with 3 being trained practitioners and the others being self-taught; 5 photographers have gained subsequent commercial collaborations, exhibition opportunities, and sales through this project so far.
