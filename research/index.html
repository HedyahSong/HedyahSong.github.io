Research

My research sits between anthropology, contemporary art and curatorial practice, with particular interests in participation, everyday life and situated forms of knowledge.

PhD research · University of Leeds

Everyday Lives and Practices Among Women in Nujiang: A Participatory Arts-Based Study in the Context of Regional Development

The project examines everyday life among women in Nujiang Lisu Autonomous Prefecture, Yunnan, through participatory arts-based and ethnographic methods. It asks how exhibitions, residencies and shared making might operate not simply as ways to present research, but as environments in which knowledge is produced through encounter.

The research brings visual anthropology into dialogue with feminist and participatory approaches, while attending to questions of representation, agency and regional development in Southwest China.
